Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mossy Labs ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile applications we publish, including AC & Fan Remote Control (the "Apps"). If you have questions, contact us at support.lumalabs@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

Our Apps do not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Settings you create in an App, such as the remotes you add, device names and preferences, are stored only on your device. We do not upload them to our own servers.

Our Apps include third-party services that automatically collect some information so that ads can be shown and so that we can understand how the Apps perform:

DataCollected byPurpose
Advertising ID and other device identifiersGoogle AdMob, Google FirebaseAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Approximate location (derived from IP address)Google AdMob, Google FirebaseAdvertising, analytics
App interactions (screens viewed, features used)Google Firebase Analytics, Google AdMobAnalytics, advertising
Crash logs and diagnosticsGoogle FirebaseFixing bugs and improving stability
Device information (model, OS version, language)Google AdMob, Google FirebaseAnalytics, ad delivery

2. Device permissions

  • Infrared transmitter: used to send remote-control signals to your appliances. No data is collected through it.
  • Internet and network state: used to load ads and send analytics and crash reports.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob and Firebase as described above.

If a future version of an App supports Wi-Fi or Bluetooth devices, any additional permission will be used only to find and control your devices on your local network or nearby.

3. How information is used and shared

We use the information above to show ads, measure how the Apps are used, fix crashes and improve features. We do not sell personal information.

Information is shared with, and processed by, the third-party services listed below under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics): firebase.google.com/support/privacy

We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. Advertising and your choices

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice from the App's settings at any time.
  • If you opt out, you may still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

5. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Data collected by Google services is kept according to their retention settings (Firebase Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months). To request deletion of data associated with your device, email us at support.lumalabs@gmail.com and we will help where we can.

6. Security

Data sent by the third-party services in our Apps is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

7. Children

Our Apps are not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through one of our Apps, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the effective date above will be updated.
